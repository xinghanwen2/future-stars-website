A student-led baseball development project founded by Vincent Hall.

FROM ONE PLAYER TO THE NEXT

Share what you learn.

Future Stars grew from Vincent’s work with younger players, beginning in his freshman year of high school. The club was formally registered in July 2025.

Today, the project combines coaching, video review, and practical drills to help young players understand their swing.
