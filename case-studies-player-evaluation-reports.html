How to Read These Reports

From Video Review to Training Direction

Each report helps turn swing video into a clear explanation of what the player is doing well, what needs to improve, and which drills can help the player move forward.

Future versions can compare multiple sessions for the same player, including baseline results, retest results, and parent or coach feedback.

What Each Report May Include

  • Player evaluation summary
  • Video-based swing observations
  • Key mechanical findings
  • Training recommendations
  • Suggested drills
  • Follow-up goals
  • Progress tracking over time
  • Parent feedback when available
