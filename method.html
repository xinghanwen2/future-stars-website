OUR METHOD

Watch. Learn. Practice. Repeat.

Simple steps toward a better understanding of each swing.

01RecordFor a matched evaluation, record side and back views of tee work and front toss. Aim for ten continuous swings in each set.
02ReviewLook at balance, timing, bat path, and contact.
03PracticeFocus on two or three targeted drills.
04RevisitRetest under comparable camera, drill, and scoring conditions. Keep dated records so a later session can be matched to its baseline.

Scoring: Good = 3 points; Medium = 2; Poor = 1. CQS = total points ÷ 30 × 100. These subjective contact categories are separate from mechanical issue frequencies and from the app’s six-category swing rubric.

The wider project includes 12 youth players and seven documented baseline-to-retest reviews. The retest reports use a six-category mechanical rubric: each category is scored out of 10, for a total of 60. Earlier baseline findings for six players were retrospectively mapped to that rubric.
