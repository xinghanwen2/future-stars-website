GROWING TOGETHER

More than a coach.

“He looks up to Vincent almost as if Vincent were his own older brother, not just his baseball coach.”

A baseball parent

Read full story

My child had never received any systematic baseball training before and started playing Arcadia PONY Baseball about two years ago completely from scratch. We feel very fortunate that Vincent has been coaching him from the beginning.

What we appreciate most about Vincent is his patience and attention to detail. He does not simply tell my child to "swing better"; he carefully identifies specific areas that my child can understand and work on. For example, Vincent recently noticed that my child's hands were starting too close to his body, causing his bat path to wrap around his body rather than extend through the ball. He also identified my child's timing issues during flips and recommended more practice with moving pitches, while teaching my child to focus on one adjustment at a time and self-check his swing. This kind of detailed feedback has helped my child better understand not only what he needs to improve, but also how to improve it.

More importantly, over the past two years my child has developed a very special relationship with Vincent. My child feels very comfortable spending time practicing with him, trusts his advice, and genuinely enjoys learning from him. He looks up to Vincent almost as if Vincent were his own older brother, not just his baseball coach. As a parent, I believe that kind of trust and connection is especially valuable for a young player. It makes my child more willing to listen, learn, practice, and keep working when something is difficult.

My child has come a long way from a child who started baseball with no experience. We truly appreciate Vincent's patience, encouragement, technical guidance, and continued commitment to helping my child grow both as a baseball player and as a young person. We are very grateful that Vincent has been part of my child's baseball journey from the very beginning.
