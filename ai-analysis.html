How It Works

AI Report Workflow

1. Upload Side View

The side view helps review stance, load, stride, contact posture, and finish.

2. Upload Back View

The back view helps review bat path, direction, timing, and plate coverage.

3. AI Review

The AI summarizes strengths, improvement areas, and possible training focus.

4. Download Report

The user can download a simple report and save it for follow-up training.

Report Sections

What the AI report will include

  • Player video summary
  • Side-view observations
  • Back-view observations
  • Main strengths
  • Main improvement areas
  • Suggested drills
  • 7–10 day practice plan
  • Retest recommendation

Privacy Reminder

Full raw videos and private reports should be stored in private Google Drive folders. Public website pages should only show approved clips, screenshots, summaries, and redacted reports.
