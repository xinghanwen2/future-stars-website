How It Works

AI Report Workflow

1. Upload Side View

The side view helps review stance, load, stride, contact posture, and finish.

2. Upload Back View

The back view helps review bat path, direction, timing, and plate coverage.

3. AI Review

The backend extracts frames and sends them to the AI model for visual analysis.

4. Save Report

The user can copy the report, download it as text, print it, or download a PDF with selected frames.

Clip Helper

Trim a Short Video Clip

Upload a longer video, choose start and end times, and the backend will trim it with FFmpeg. The downloaded MP4 can be used as your side-view or back-view upload.

Recommended: Trim one clear swing to about 3–8 seconds. The backend allows up to 30 seconds, but shorter clips are better for speed and AI quality.

Privacy Reminder

Full raw videos and private reports should be stored in private Google Drive folders. Public website pages should only show approved clips, screenshots, summaries, and redacted reports.
